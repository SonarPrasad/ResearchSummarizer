Our Mission

Welcome to Research Summarizer! We aim to simplify the research process for students, academics, and professionals by offering an AI-powered tool for quick summarization of research papers and reports.

Features

  • Upload and summarize PDF research documents.
  • AI-driven question-answering and project report generation.
  • Responsive, user-friendly interface with secure document management.
  • Available on multiple platforms for enhanced productivity.

Our Goal

Our goal is to make research more accessible and to help students and researchers focus on what really matters: the content. By automating summarization and providing a platform for managing documents, Research Summarizer allows users to save time and work more efficiently.
